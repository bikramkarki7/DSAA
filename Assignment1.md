
## Difference Between KDD and Data Mining

| KDD (Knowledge Discovery in Database) | Data Mining |
|---|---|
| KDD is the complete process of finding useful knowledge from data. | Data mining is the process of finding useful patterns from data. |
| It involves several steps, such as data cleaning, selection, transformation, and evaluation. | It mainly focuses on discovering hidden patterns in data. |
| KDD is a broader process. | Data mining is a part of KDD. |
| Its main goal is to convert raw data into useful knowledge. | Its main goal is to find interesting patterns and relationships. |
| It includes data mining as one of its important steps. | It is one step within the KDD process. |
| Example: Finding customer buying habits from a company's database. | Example: Finding which products customers often buy together. |
